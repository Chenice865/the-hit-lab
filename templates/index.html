What is Prescriptive Analytics?

Prescriptive analytics goes beyond analysing data by recommending actions based on available information. In The Hit Lab, it recommends songs with statistical characteristics similar to the selected track, helping users discover what to listen to next.

Prescriptive analytics helps users decide what to listen to next by recommending songs with statistical characteristics similar to the selected track. The Hit Lab compares popularity, artist popularity, song duration, album type and explicit status to identify the closest matches.

Recommendation Method

The selected track is compared with every other song in the dataset using The Hit Lab's existing similarity model. The model evaluates:

  • Track popularity
  • Artist popularity
  • Song duration
  • Album type
  • Explicit status

The songs with the highest overall similarity scores are presented as the recommended tracks.
